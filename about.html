Our story

Flowers you build, keep and give

PosyBlocks started with a simple idea: the best flowers are the ones still there next month. Our bouquets are building-block stems you put together yourself, so the gift is both a relaxing project and a decoration that lasts for years.

We are a small independent shop based in Türkiye, shipping to customers in the USA with free tracked delivery through our fulfilment partner.
