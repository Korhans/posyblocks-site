Returns & refunds

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

Missing or damaged parts: email posyblocks@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the problem. We will send replacement parts free of charge or, if that is not possible, refund the affected item.

Change of mind: you may return unopened items within 14 days of delivery. Email us first to receive the return address. Return postage is paid by the customer. Your refund for the item price is issued within 5 business days after we receive the parcel.

Opened sets, built stems and the Build Your Own Bouquet cannot be returned for change of mind, but are always covered for missing or damaged parts.

Cancellations: you can cancel free of charge, with a full refund, any time before your order is dispatched. Email us with your order number.

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Shopier. Your bank may take a few additional days to show the credit.
