Good to know

Questions

Can’t find your answer? Email posyblocks@gmail.com and we will reply within 24 hours.

Are the flowers already built?

No. You receive the bricks and instructions for every stem and build them yourself. Building is part of the fun and part of the gift.

Is a vase included?

No. Vases, wrapping paper, ribbons and decorations in our photos are styling only. The stems fit standard bud vases and jars.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 1–3 business days. Delivery to the USA typically takes 7–15 business days, and you get a tracking number by email when your order ships.

Do you ship outside the USA?

Our free shipping and delivery times are for US addresses. For other countries, email us before ordering and we will confirm whether we can ship to you and what it costs.

A piece is missing or broken. What now?

Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send replacement parts or refund you.

Is it suitable for children?

The sets contain small parts and are recommended for ages 14 and up. They are not suitable for children under 3.

Which currency will I pay in?

Checkout is handled securely by Shopier. Your card issuer converts the amount to your own currency, so the final figure on your statement can differ slightly from the USD price shown here.

How do I choose flowers for Build Your Own?

Use the bouquet builder to pick six stems and add the bouquet to your cart. At checkout we show your stem list: paste it into the order note on Shopier, or email it with your order number within 24 hours.

How does the bundle deal work?

Put any two or more items in your cart, in any mix, and 10% comes off your whole order. The discount is applied automatically in the Shopier cart when you pay.
